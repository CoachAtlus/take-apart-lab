How to play

  • Spin it: drag on empty space. Drag up or down to look from above or underneath.
  • Zoom: pinch, or use the + and − buttons. Zooming in centers on the last piece you touched.
  • Take it apart: drag a piece off with your finger (or tap it).
  • Move a piece: drag a loose piece around the table. Tap it to put it back.
  • Look inside: tap Look inside. Everything glowing blue has an inside. Tap one to shrink down inside it, then keep going deeper. Back climbs out.
  • Learn more: tap Tell me more to go deeper, from kid level all the way to grown-up science.
  • Sparky: the robot says what to do next. Tap him to hear it again. The hand button turns his help on or off.

Take-Apart Lab

Pick something to open up!

How to play
  • Drag to spin it around.
  • Pinch or scroll to zoom.
  • Drag a part with your finger to pull it out. Tap it to put it back.
  • Tap a board you pulled out to look closer and take its pieces off.
  • Sparky the robot tells you what to do next. Tap him to hear it again.
  • Turn it on, then pull parts out and see what breaks!
Safety first Real TVs can keep a big zap inside even when they are unplugged. Take apart real things only with a grown-up.

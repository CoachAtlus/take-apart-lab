# Improvements checklist

The bar every view should meet:

- Pulling any piece visibly changes something, or says plainly that nothing changed and what the part is for.
- Every view has something to *do*, not just watch.
- Explanations go 3 to 5 levels deep, plain words first.
- Things look like the real material.
- Nothing is cut off or overlapping on an iPad.

The first list came from an audit of every part and inside view on 2026-11-14. Most of it was built the same day. What's left is at the bottom.

## Done (2026-11-14)

- [x] **Quick fixes:**
  - Take apart is hidden where nothing comes off.
  - No more "magnifying glass" wording.
  - Name tags are bigger, nudged apart and kept above the toolbar.
  - The Look inside badge stays clear of the caption.
  - The picture tube inside view is brighter, with visible beams.
  - The color dots get thick beams.
  - Put back no longer crashes at Beginner.
- [x] **Critical path:**
  - Every pull says what broke, or "Still works!" plus what the part is for.
  - Real breakage was added:
    - LED TV: power button, tuner, HDMI;
    - tube TV: volume knob;
    - flashlight: lens;
    - chip: cores, heat paste.
  - **Must-have parts** mode colors each part red (stops it), orange (changes it) or green (can go), judged against the fully built device.
- [x] **An experiment in every inside view:**
  - current dials on the LEDs;
  - gate voltage on the transistor;
  - cores and heat on the chip;
  - charge and discharge on the capacitor;
  - pitch and loudness on the speaker;
  - coil turns on the transformer;
  - beam steering on the picture tube;
  - beam mixing on the dots of color;
  - bands on the resistor;
  - melt and protect on the fuse;
  - heat and knock on the magnet;
  - workload on the heat sink;
  - flipping the diode;
  - pressing the switch;
  - wire thickness;
  - pushing electrons through copper;
  - turning the polarizer sheet;
  - mixing on the color filter;
  - tightening the screw;
  - cutting a copper road on the board;
  - electricity levels on the liquid crystal;
  - the zinc reaction in the battery.
- [x] **Explanations:**
  - Every part has 3+ levels.
  - Every inside view and device has "tell me more" on its intro.
  - Each part keeps one analogy across its levels.
- [x] **Checked by several models:**
  - One model checked about 900 claims against sources and fixed 32.
  - A second, different model re-checked them. See `VERIFICATION.md`.
- [x] **Looks:**

- [x] **Games:**
